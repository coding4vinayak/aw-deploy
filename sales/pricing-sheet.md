## What the plans include

All five paid apps share the same commercial shape:

- 14-day free trial with card; after the trial the plan converts automatically
  unless canceled in the portal
- Full product — no artificial feature gates between trial and paid
- Email notifications at every billing transition: trial ending, converted,
  payment recovered, canceled. Cancellation keeps the workspace and invites the
  buyer back; the four queue-backed apps also send a 7-day win-back mail.

## Buying

- **One app**: go to its `/pricing`, pick monthly or yearly, pay by card
  (Stripe). Self-serve, no call needed.
- **More than one app**: buy each app standalone — there is **no published
  bundle discount**, and don't invent one on a call. If a prospect wants
  several, the honest line is: *"they're separate products; start with the one
  that hurts most, add the next when its trial ends."*
- **The free infrastructure** (AbetFlow, AbetAPI) needs no purchase: create an
  account and use the docs. Their pricing pages say "$0 — included with the
  Abetworks suite."
- **Enterprise / annual invoicing**: the yearly option is the invoice path
  ("two months free, one invoice").
